Ratatoskr fleet map

Seventeen repositories, pinned together by ratatoskr-workspace. Read from top to bottom like the tree the squirrel runs on: the surfaces people touch, the platform trunk that carries every event, the bounded contexts on its branches, and the wire contracts at the roots. Every arrow points from a repository to what it relies on.

Source: workspace.toml (dependencies, profiles, contracts) and each child README.md at the pins of workspace main (b410b09), plus the port table in docs/DEPLOYMENT_TARGET.md. Solid lines are manifest dependencies. Dashed lines are relationships the READMEs describe that the manifest does not list.
